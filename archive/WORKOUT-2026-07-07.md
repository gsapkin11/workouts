# 4-DAY WORKOUT SPLIT

*Last updated: 2026-07-07 · Canonical copy lives in Notion ("💪🏽 Workouts" page); this file is the version history for the biweekly refresh.*

**Focus:** Strength, Reps & Leanness | **Duration:** ~45 min per session
**Schedule:** Upper A → Lower A → Upper B → Lower B → Rest → Rest → Rest
**Progression:** Main compounds stay 4-6 weeks | Accessories + core rotate every 2 weeks | Deload every 4th week

---

## WARMUP (8 min)

*Dynamic stretches that activate as you move*

- [ ] **Inchworm to Push-Up** — 6 reps
    - *Walk hands out, do 1 push-up, walk feet to hands, stand*
    - *Activates: chest, shoulders, core, hamstrings*
- [ ] **World's Greatest Stretch** — 5/side
    - *Deep lunge → elbow to floor → rotate and reach → straighten leg*
    - *Activates: hip flexors, glutes, thoracic spine, hamstrings*
- [ ] **Glute Bridge with March** — 10 reps (5/leg)
    - *Hold bridge, lift one foot off ground, alternate*
    - *Activates: glutes, core*
- [ ] **Quadruped Thoracic Rotation** — 8/side
    - *Hand behind head, rotate elbow to ceiling, follow with eyes*
    - *Activates: thoracic spine, obliques*
- [ ] **Leg Swings (front/back + side/side)** — 10 each direction/leg
    - *Controlled swing, full range*
    - *Activates: hip flexors, glutes, adductors*
- [ ] **Bodyweight Squat with Reach** — 10 reps
    - *Squat down, reach arms overhead at bottom, stand*
    - *Activates: quads, glutes, shoulders*
- [ ] **Push-Up to Down Dog** — 6 reps
    - *Push-up, pike hips up, press heels down, return to push-up*
    - *Activates: chest, shoulders, calves, hamstrings*
- [ ] **Dead Bug** — 8/side
    - *Low back pressed down, opposite arm/leg extend*
    - *Activates: deep core*

## Day 1: Upper A (Chest Emphasis)

### Warmup (10 min)
*(See Consistent Warmup above)*

### Main Workout (24 min)

**Chest (Primary)**
- [ ] A: Barbell Bench Press *(KEEP 4-6 weeks)* — 4x6-8 (90s rest) — 7 min
    - *Retract shoulder blades, feet flat, touch chest, drive through chest not shoulders*
- [ ] B1: Low-to-High Cable Fly — 3x12
    - *Cables set low, bring hands up and together, squeeze upper chest*
- [ ] B2: Dip (chest focus) — 3x10 (60s rest) — 5 min
    - *Lean forward, go deep, elbows flare slightly, feel chest stretch*

**Back (Secondary)**
- [ ] C: Single-Arm Cable Row — 3x10/arm (45s rest) — 3 min
    - *Pull to hip, squeeze lat, control the return, no rotation*

**Shoulders**
- [ ] D1: Standing Dumbbell Press — 3x10
    - *Core braced, press straight up, don't arch back*
- [ ] D2: Leaning Lateral Raise (cable) — 3x12/arm (45s rest) — 4 min
    - *Hold pole, lean away, raise to shoulder height, constant tension*

**Arms**
- [ ] E1: Skull Crushers (EZ bar) — 3x12
    - *Lower to forehead, elbows stay tight, extend fully*
- [ ] E2: Cable Curl (straight bar) — 3x12 (45s rest) — 4 min
    - *Elbows pinned, squeeze at top, control down*

### Core + Cardio Finisher (11 min)
- [ ] Toes to Bar — 3x10 (15s rest) — 2 min
    - *Control swing, bring toes all the way up, slow negative*
- [ ] Cable Lift (low to high) — 3x10/side (15s rest) — 2 min
    - *Arms straight, rotate from hips, core does the work*
- [ ] Bike, Treadmill, or Stairmaster — 8 min

**Total: ~45 min**

## Day 2: Lower A (Quad Emphasis + Hamstring Secondary)

### Warmup (10 min)
*(See Consistent Warmup above)*

### Main Workout (25 min)

**Quads (Primary)**
- [ ] A: Barbell Back Squat *(KEEP 4-6 weeks)* — 4x6-8 (90s rest) — 7 min
    - *Bar on upper traps, brace core, sit deep, drive through whole foot*
- [ ] B1: Walking Lunge (DB) — 3x10/leg
    - *Long stride, front knee tracks over toe, push through front heel*
- [ ] B2: Leg Extension — 3x15 (60s rest) — 5 min
    - *Squeeze hard at top, slow negative, don't swing*

**Hamstrings (Secondary)**
- [ ] C: Seated Leg Curl — 3x12 (45s rest) — 3 min
    - *Squeeze hard at bottom, slow 3s negative*
- [ ] D: Single-Leg RDL (DB) — 3x8/leg (60s rest) — 4 min
    - *Hinge at hip, slight knee bend, back stays flat*

**Adductors/Abductors**
- [ ] E1: Cable Adduction — 3x12/leg
- [ ] E2: Cable Abduction — 3x12/leg (45s rest) — 3 min
    - *Stand tall, control both directions, squeeze at end range*

**Calves**
- [ ] F: Leg Press Calf Raise — 3x15 (45s rest) — 3 min
    - *Full stretch at bottom, pause at top, no bouncing*

### Core + Cardio Finisher (11 min)
- [ ] Half Kneeling Cable Chop — 3x10/side (15s rest) — 2 min
    - *Hips stay square, rotate through core, exhale on chop*
- [ ] Copenhagen Plank — 2x20s/side (15s rest) — 2 min
    - *Top leg on bench, bottom leg floats, stay stacked*
- [ ] Bike, Treadmill, or Stairmaster — 8 min

**Total: ~46 min**

## Day 3: Upper B (Back Emphasis)

### Warmup (10 min)
*(See Consistent Warmup above)*

### Main Workout (24 min)

**Back (Primary)**
- [ ] A: Weighted Pull-Up *(KEEP 4-6 weeks)* — 4x6-8 (75s rest) — 6 min
    - *Full hang at bottom, pull chest to bar, control descent*
- [ ] B1: T-Bar Row — 3x10
    - *Hinge at hips, pull to chest, squeeze shoulder blades*
- [ ] B2: Straight-Arm Pulldown — 3x12 (60s rest) — 5 min
    - *Arms stay straight, pull with lats, pause at bottom*

**Chest (Secondary)**
- [ ] C: Push-Up (weighted or elevated feet) — 3x15 (45s rest) — 3 min
    - *Core tight, chest to floor, full lockout*

**Shoulders**
- [ ] D1: Landmine Press (standing) — 3x10/arm
    - *Staggered stance, press up and out, core braced*
- [ ] D2: Reverse Pec Deck — 3x15 (45s rest) — 4 min
    - *Lead with elbows, squeeze rear delts, control return*

**Arms**
- [ ] E1: Incline Dumbbell Curl — 3x12
    - *Arms hang straight, full stretch at bottom, no swinging*
- [ ] E2: Close-Grip Bench Press — 3x10 (45s rest) — 4 min
    - *Hands shoulder-width, elbows tucked, feel triceps*

### Core + Cardio Finisher (11 min)
- [ ] Hanging Windshield Wipers — 3x6/side (20s rest) — 2 min
    - *Legs straight, rotate side to side with control*
- [ ] Stir the Pot — 3x8/direction (15s rest) — 2 min
    - *Forearms on ball, make circles, keep hips still*
- [ ] Bike, Treadmill, or Stairmaster — 8 min

**Total: ~45 min**

## Day 4: Lower B (Posterior Emphasis + Quad Secondary)

### Warmup (10 min)
*(See Consistent Warmup above)*

### Main Workout (25 min)

**Hamstrings (Primary)**
- [ ] A: Trap Bar Deadlift *(KEEP 4-6 weeks)* — 4x6-8 (90s rest) — 7 min
    - *Grip center handles, chest up, drive through floor, hinge and lower*
- [ ] B1: Lying Leg Curl — 3x12
    - *Squeeze hard at bottom, don't lift hips*
- [ ] B2: Nordic Curl (assisted) — 3x6-8 (60s rest) — 5 min
    - *Lower as slow as possible, use hands to push back up*

**Glutes (Primary)**
- [ ] C: Cable Pull-Through *(KEEP 4-6 weeks)* — 4x12-15 (60s rest) — 5 min
    - *Hinge at hips, squeeze glutes hard at top, don't hyperextend*

**Quads (Secondary)**
- [ ] D: Front Foot Elevated Split Squat — 3x10/leg (60s rest) — 4 min
    - *Front foot on plate, sit deep, drive through front heel*

**Hip Flexors**
- [ ] E: Hanging Knee Raise with Pause — 3x10 (45s rest) — 2 min
    - *Curl pelvis up, pause 2s at top, control descent*

**Calves**
- [ ] F: Donkey Calf Raise — 3x15 (45s rest) — 3 min
    - *Full stretch at bottom, squeeze hard at top*

### Core + Cardio Finisher (11 min)
- [ ] Medicine Ball Rotational Slam — 3x8/side (15s rest) — 2 min
    - *Power from hips, slam hard, catch and repeat*
- [ ] Farmer's Carry — 2x40s (15s rest) — 2 min
    - *Heavy dumbbells, shoulders back, core braced, walk steady*
- [ ] Bike, Treadmill, or Stairmaster — 8 min

**Total: ~46 min**

## Deload Protocol (Every 4th Week)

| Adjustment | How |
|------------|-----|
| Volume | Cut sets in half |
| Intensity | 50-60% of normal weight |
| Core | 1 exercise instead of 2 |
| Cardio | Keep at 8 min, low intensity |

scheduled routine. Each refresh:

   4-6 weeks per the progression rules above)
2. Replaces the content of the Notion "💪🏽 Workouts" page
3. Archives the previous version of this file to `archive/` and commits the
   new one here
